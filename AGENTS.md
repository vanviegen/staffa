## Changelog

Add anything a user of the library would notice — a new component or option, changed behaviour, a fix, a removal — to `CHANGELOG.md`, under the version being prepared. A sentence or two each, in plain language; mark **breaking** changes and say how to migrate. Skip refactors, internals and tests.

## Commit messages

One short line. Prefix a release commit with its version: `v0.9.0: Panel navigation improvements`.
